# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participate with respect, patience, and technical honesty. Welcome questions, give actionable
feedback, credit others' work, and disagree with ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior harms the project or its participants. Report conduct concerns privately
to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect privacy where practical, and choose a response
proportionate to the behavior and its impact.
